# Sahayak — User Personas

Version: 1.0

This document defines the primary users of Sahayak.

Every feature, page, interaction, and workflow should be designed for real human needs rather than generic application requirements.

The platform exists to connect people seeking guidance with experienced mentors.

Always optimize for trust, clarity, and meaningful conversations.

---

# Primary User Groups

The application serves two major groups.

1. Seekers

People looking for guidance.

2. Mentors

People willing to share knowledge.

Every feature should clearly identify which user group it serves.

# Design Implications

Students expect modern interfaces.

Senior citizens expect simplicity.

Professionals expect credibility.

Farmers expect accessibility.

Doctors expect professionalism.

Parents expect empathy.

The design should satisfy all groups without becoming complicated.

---

# Product Principle

The application should never make users feel

Lost.

Confused.

Overwhelmed.

Instead, every interaction should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

---

# Success Definition

A successful experience means

The seeker found the right mentor.

The mentor shared valuable knowledge.

Both users are willing to return.

Every feature should move users closer to this outcome.
