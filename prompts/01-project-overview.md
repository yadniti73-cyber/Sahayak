# Sahayak — Project Overview

## Project Name

**Sahayak**

Meaning:
Sahayak means "Helper" in Hindi.

The platform exists to connect people who seek guidance with trusted people willing to provide it.

Unlike traditional marketplaces, Sahayak focuses on human knowledge, mentorship, and life guidance rather than buying and selling products.

---

# Vision

Everyone reaches moments where they need advice.

A student choosing a career.

A farmer facing crop disease.

A parent raising a child.

A patient confused after a diagnosis.

An entrepreneur starting a company.

An artist trying to find an audience.

A retired professional wishing to give back.

Sahayak exists to make experienced people accessible.

Knowledge should never remain locked inside one person.

---

# Mission

Build India's largest mentorship ecosystem where every individual can find verified guidance within minutes.

The platform should remove barriers between experience and curiosity by enabling meaningful conversations through trusted experts.

---

# Core Philosophy

People don't always need information.

They need confidence.

Search engines provide answers.

Sahayak provides people.

The platform must always emphasize trust, empathy, and authentic human interaction over transactions.

---

# Product Goals

The application should allow users to:

• Discover mentors across multiple life domains

• Book one-on-one sessions

• Connect online or offline

• Build long-term mentor relationships

• Share knowledge with society
